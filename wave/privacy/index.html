Wave — Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Wave ("the app") is a meditation application made by monoscale. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Data We Collect

None.

Wave does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. Everything the app stores stays on your device:

  • Your meditation session history (date and duration)
  • Your settings (session length, sound, vibration, reminder preferences)
  • Whether you have completed onboarding

This data is stored in your device's local storage (UserDefaults) and is never sent to any server or third party.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, Wave schedules local notifications on your device. These notifications are generated entirely on-device — no data about them is sent anywhere.

Third-Party Services

Wave uses no analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or any other third-party SDK. There are no third parties involved in operating this app.

Children

Wave is rated 4+ and contains no content inappropriate for children. The app does not collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
